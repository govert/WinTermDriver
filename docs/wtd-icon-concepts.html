WTD Icon Concepts

Two directions: split-pane terminal glyphs for product clarity, and W/cursor monograms for a more compact app identity. Each card shows a 96px preview plus 48, 32, and 16px checks.

Split Pane Glyph

A. Classic Pane Grid clear product signal
Window frame, active pane, prompt, and cursor. Most literal and easiest to explain.
B. Active Bottom Pane taskbar friendly
Slightly simpler geometry and stronger active cursor for small sizes.
C. Light Tile Windows-adjacent
Cleaner, brighter direction. Works if the app icon should stand apart from dark terminals.
D. Rounded Console warm terminal
Softer pane framing with terminal prompt retained. Good for a friendly dev tool.

W Monogram + Cursor

E. Stroke W strong at 16px
Bold monogram with terminal block cursor. Smallest-size readability is best here.
F. Filled W app-mark feel
Less terminal-like, more brand-like. Holds shape well as a favicon.
G. Monospace W quiet utility
Built from terminal-like vertical strokes. Feels calmer and more tool-like.
H. Terminal Top Bar hybrid cue
Monogram plus subtle terminal window line. More identifiable as a terminal app.
